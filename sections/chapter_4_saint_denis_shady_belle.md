# Sections -- Chapter 4: Saint Denis (camp: Shady Belle)

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-10-30
**research_run:** P1 2026-10-30; P2 2026-10-30

Context: the gang hunts for a kidnapped child in Saint Denis. The Fandom wiki titles this chapter "Saint Denis"; its camp and the in-game Journal label are "Shady Belle". Gated by the player's progress ceiling (Chapter 1-2).

- Missions: The Joys of Civilization; No, No and Thrice, No; Angelo Bronte, A Man of Honor; Help a Brother Out; Horsemen, Apocalypses; Urban Pleasures; Country Pursuits; Revenge is a Dish Best Eaten; Banking, The Old American Art; The Gilded Cage; Fatherhood and Other Dreams; A Fine Night of Debauchery; American Fathers I-II; Brothers and Sisters, One and All; Money Lending and Other Sins V; and one cutscene mission. The wiki counts 16 missions including 4 optional.
