# Red Dead Redemption 2 -- Settings

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-10-30
**research_run:** P1 2026-10-30

The in-game menu tab names were not confirmed by any source read, so the entries below are grouped by topic and carry no menu paths. [unverified: exact tab names and menu paths] Motion blur and aim-assist or targeting-mode settings were not recovered. [gap]

- FOV: sliders with 11 positions for third- and first-person.

- Colorblind mode: Protanopia, Deuteranopia, Tritanopia.
_source: https://www.pcgamingwiki.com/wiki/Red_Dead_Redemption_2 (P1 research 2026-10-30) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: none_ [Single source -- verify · class:community-wiki]

- Subtitles can be limited to Mission Only; a Speaker Name toggle exists; non-speech sounds (horses, music) are not captioned.
_source: https://www.pcgamingwiki.com/wiki/Red_Dead_Redemption_2 (P1 research 2026-10-30) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: none_ [Single source -- verify · class:community-wiki]

- HDR: "Game" mode is full HDR; "Cinematic" is scaled SDR and looks washed out. Windows HDR must be on before launch under DX12; under Vulkan HDR works only in fullscreen.
_source: https://www.pcgamingwiki.com/wiki/Red_Dead_Redemption_2 (P1 research 2026-10-30) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: none_ [Single source -- verify · class:community-wiki]

- Upscalers: DLSS 2.2 and FSR 2.2 (FSR was updated in Title Update 1.32).
_source: https://www.pcgamingwiki.com/wiki/Red_Dead_Redemption_2 ; https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4GR27mnSMJaPqF5gl2zDNu/red-dead-redemption-2-title-update-1-32-notes-ps4-xbox-one-pc (P1 research 2026-10-30) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: none_ [Confirmed: 2 sources, 1 language]

- Above 30 FPS some cloth physics "jump"; this is a perception issue, not a difficulty one.
_source: https://www.pcgamingwiki.com/wiki/Red_Dead_Redemption_2 (P1 research 2026-10-30) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: none_ [Single source -- verify · class:community-wiki]

- Vulkan with resizable BAR causes a VRAM leak; fixes are to disable ReBAR in the BIOS, disable triple buffering, or use DX12.
_source: https://www.pcgamingwiki.com/wiki/Red_Dead_Redemption_2 (P1 research 2026-10-30) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: none_ [Single source -- verify · class:community-wiki]

- AMD: an ERR_GFX_STATE crash can happen when detonating dynamite in a Chapter 2 stranger mission; set Particle Quality to low, medium or ultra.
_source: https://www.pcgamingwiki.com/wiki/Red_Dead_Redemption_2 (P1 research 2026-10-30) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_ [Single source -- verify · class:community-wiki]

- Save slots: 1 autosave plus 15 manual.
_source: https://www.pcgamingwiki.com/wiki/Red_Dead_Redemption_2 (P1 research 2026-10-30) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: none_ [Single source -- verify · class:community-wiki]
