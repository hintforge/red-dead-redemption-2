## Current status

Mechanical moral meter that moves with the player's choices. Pointers only; claims live in their primary files.

## Capabilities

- High Honor gives store discounts and special outfits; low Honor gives more loot: `mechanics.md`.
- Honor missions are optional, chapter-bound missions counted by the "Lending a Hand" achievement; one needs Honor level 4: `mechanics.md`, `sections/chapter_6_beaver_hollow.md`, `achievements.md`.

## Achievements

- "Extreme Personality" needs maximum or minimum Honor; it cannot unlock before a late story mission: `mechanics.md`, `achievements.md`.

## Missability

- Honor missions expire with their chapters; documented exception: "Good, Honest, Snake Oil" lasts until a late chapter. The mission list is disputed between sources (15 or 19): `mechanics.md`, `sections/missables.md`.
- Absent from camp more than three days costs Arthur Honor on return: `nav/architecture.md`.
