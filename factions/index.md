## Why this file exists

This file is the corpus's roster for group entities the player has a relationship with -- gangs, law-enforcement groups and town powers. Groups beyond the player's current chapter (1-2) stay hidden by the spoiler ceiling in `warning_tiers.md`.

No faction facts were captured with enough content for their own entity files at P1 or P2; class folder retained pending downstream-phase research. The research names these groups only in passing (one line each, in Chapter 1-6 enemy notes and the compendium): O'Driscoll Boys, Murfree Brood, Night Folk, Lemoyne Raiders, and two later-chapter groups whose names are withheld here as spoilers. Chapter files carry what was found.
