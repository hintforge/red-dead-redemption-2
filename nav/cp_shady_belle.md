**Type:** main-story zone (Chapter 4 camp, a manor in Bayou Nwa, south Lemoyne; Saint Denis focus)
**Linear?:** yes (with optional branches)
**Chapter:** 4 (Fandom wiki title "Saint Denis"; the GTABase mission list and the in-game Journal say "Shady Belle")

## Entry

From `cp_clemens_point` via E3. Gated by the player's progress ceiling (Chapter 1-2).

## Exit

To `isl_guarma` via E4, by finishing "Banking, The Old American Art" (story-gate, one-way).
**Type:** story-gate
**Point of no return:** permanent. Closes: "Give to the Poor", "Errand Boy" and "Friends With Benefits" windows; the camp ledger, donations and upgrades (Shady Belle is the last camp that has them); Chapter 4 companion activities (hunting with Pearson, coach robbery with Lenny or Micah, rustling with Uncle, bank robbery with Charles); camp chores for Arthur.

## Outgoing edges

## Entry tips (3 max)

1. Finish everything camp-related (requests, companion activities, donations, Ledger purchases) before the last mission of this chapter.
2. The exotics strand and a bounty chain open here; some Chapter 6 missions depend on two Chapter 4 strangers.
3. Do not kill a particular bank-hideout target in the sixth mission if you want his later bounty.

## Optional branches / shortcuts

- The vampire stranger in Saint Denis and the Bronte bridge guards are Chapter 4 content; keep a low wanted level near the bridges.

## Common confusions

- "Shady Belle" is the camp (Chapter 4), the mission "The Battle of Shady Belle" (end of Chapter 3) and, in some mission lists, the chapter title.
