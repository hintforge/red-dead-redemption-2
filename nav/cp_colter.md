## Entry

Game start. The camp is the abandoned mining town of Colter in Grizzlies West, near Spider Gorge, west Ambarino (zone ids: chapter `ch1_colter`, town `tn_colter`, region `rg_grizzlies_west`; see the name-collision note in `architecture.md`).

## Exit

To `cp_horseshoe_overlook` via edge E1 (`architecture.md`), by finishing "Eastward Bound".
**Type:** story-gate
**Point of no return:** chapter-bound. Colter is never a camp again; afterwards it is reachable only as a fast-travel destination from a wilderness camp (one commenter disputes that).

## Outgoing edges

- E1 (`cp_colter`, `cp_horseshoe_overlook`) -- story-gate, finish "Eastward Bound"

## Entry tips (3 max)

1. During this chapter the storm means Arthur cannot leave camp except on certain missions without dying; do not try to explore.
2. Two missable letters sit on desks in a train carriage during the fifth mission, and the first horse is obtainable only in the first mission.
3. The donation box is introduced at the end of the last mission; the "Give to the Poor", "Errand Boy" and "Friends With Benefits" windows open after it.
