**Type:** main-story zone (Chapter 3 camp; Rhodes and Scarlett Meadows focus)
**Linear?:** yes (with optional branches)
**Chapter:** 3 Clemens Point

## Entry

From `cp_horseshoe_overlook` via E2. The camp is in Scarlett Meadows on the west shore of Flat Iron Lake, Lemoyne. Gated by the player's progress ceiling (Chapter 1-2).

## Exit

To `cp_shady_belle` via E3, by finishing "The Battle of Shady Belle" (the camp moves; one-way).
**Type:** story-gate
**Point of no return:** chapter-bound. Chapter 3 companion activities (Five Finger Fillet with Micah, dominoes with Tilly, home robbery with Sean, fishing with Javier or Kieran, the two coach robberies) and Chapter 3-only item requests (Hosea's ginseng, Kieran's burdock, Molly's mirror) close. The camp boat ledger upgrade exists only in Chapters 3 and 4.

> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-017.

## Outgoing edges

- E3 (`cp_clemens_point`, `cp_shady_belle`) -- story-gate, finish "The Battle of Shady Belle"

## Entry tips (3 max)

1. Do the Chapter 3 item requests and companion activities before the exit mission.
2. Two missions hold one-time items: a rifle carried by a bounty hunter in the Braithwaite barn ("Magicians for Sport") and a shield and letter in the manor ("Blood Feuds, Ancient and Modern").
3. High Stakes treasure-hunter encounters appear after "The New South".
