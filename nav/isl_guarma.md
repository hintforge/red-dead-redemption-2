**Type:** main-story zone (detached island, then a temporary mainland camp)
**Linear?:** yes
**Chapter:** 5. Everything in this file is `story` and gated by the player's progress ceiling (Chapter 1-2).

## Entry

From `cp_shady_belle` via E4 (one-way). The first camp is the chapel ruin La Capilla; after the return the camp is Lakay in Bayou Nwa near Lagras, Lemoyne.

## Exit

To `cp_beaver_hollow` via E5b, by finishing "That's Murfree Country" (camp moves; one-way). **Point of no return:** permanent for the island: free roam closes once both "A Kind and Benevolent Despot" and "Savagery Unleashed" are complete.

## Outgoing edges

- E5 (`isl_guarma`, `cp_lakay`) -- story-gate, finish the island missions and return
- E5b (`cp_lakay`, `cp_beaver_hollow`) -- story-gate, finish "That's Murfree Country"

## Entry tips (3 max)

1. Study and skin the island animals (macaws, boas, booby) before finishing both named island missions, because they cannot be handled afterwards; the "Skin Deep" and "Zoologist" achievements exclude the island-only species.
2. Camp table games end from this chapter.
3. Two strangers ("The Wisdom of the Elders", "The Veteran") are exceptions to "Chapter 5 content is missable" and can be played later.
