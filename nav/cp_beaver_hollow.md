**Type:** main-story zone (hill basin by a cave, Roanoke Ridge, New Hanover)
**Linear?:** yes (with many optional Honor strands)
**Chapter:** 6. Gated (late-game and story) by the player's progress ceiling (Chapter 1-2).

## Entry

From `cp_lakay` via E5b.

## Exit

To `cp_pronghorn_ranch` via E6, by finishing the chapter's last mission. **Point of no return:** point-of-divergence; some valuables do not carry over, and the old rival gang is replaced.

## Outgoing edges

- E6 (`cp_beaver_hollow`, `cp_pronghorn_ranch`) -- story-gate

## Entry tips (3 max)

1. Do the remaining Honor missions, the homestead stashes and the Willard's Rest strand before the last mission.
2. Camp fast travel is free in this chapter with no upgrade.
3. "Do Not Seek Absolution" needs Honor level 4 or higher; "Of Men and Angels" needs an earlier Saint Denis strand and must be finished before "The Fine Art of Conversation".
