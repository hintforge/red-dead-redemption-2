# Red Dead Redemption 2 -- Cigarette cards (144)

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-10-30
**research_run:** P1 2026-10-30; P2 2026-10-30

144 cards in 12 sets of 12. [location inventory incomplete]: P1 recovered no fixed world locations (the one third-party map keeps them inside a JavaScript map); P2 adds one guide's location text for every card (descriptions for two are garbled or cut off). Rows are keyed on the nearest named landmark; the region column inherits the source's wording, whose state labels are sometimes wrong. No source gives coordinates.

- Strand "Smoking and Other Hobbies" with Phineas T. Ramsbottom is available in Chapter 2 after the mission "Who is Not Without Sin"; he gives one card on meeting.

- Premium Cigarettes from any general store give one random card per pack (infinite stock); each set has one card that spawns only after you accept the strand. Mail a complete set from a post office; you must physically hold it (selling a card breaks the set). The reward for all cards is $200 (single source). The 100% checklist needs only one full set. Not missable if packs are bought after accepting the strand.
_source: https://www.powerpyx.com/red-dead-redemption-2-all-cigarette-card-locations/ ; https://www.powerpyx.com/red-dead-redemption-2-100-guide-checklist/ (P1 research 2026-10-30) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_ [Single source -- verify · class:editorial-en]

- The Robin Koninsky card (Stars of the Stage) is gated: it needs the Chapter 4 strand "Fatherhood and Other Dreams" finished and every theatre performance watched. The "Dynamite", "Coral Snake" and "Steam Locomotive" rows name places that belong to later chapters' camps or settlements. Two cards are both called "Steam Locomotive" (one in Amazing Inventions, one in Marvels of Travel) per the same guide; whether the game really has two was not cross-checked.
