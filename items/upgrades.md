# Red Dead Redemption 2 -- Upgrades

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-10-30

- Camp: the Ledger appears next to the tithing box after Leopold Strauss's first "Money Lending and Other Sins" mission (Chapter 2, unlocked after the first five missions). Upgrades include Leather Working Tools (more crafting) and a lodging upgrade that enables camp fast travel.

- P2 refines the Ledger unlock: it opens once any debt from "Money Lending and Other Sins I or II" is deposited in the donation box. The camp fast-travel map is bought as two lodging upgrades, $220 and then $325 ($545 in total; 20% off on the Special/Ultimate Edition). Details of what the map does are in `nav/architecture.md`.
