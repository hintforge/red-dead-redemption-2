## Hunting requests (5), Ms. Hobbs

- Pick up a poster at the Valentine, Strawberry, Rhodes, Saint Denis or Van Horn post office; later requests chain automatically. Mail the carcasses and collect payment after 24 hours. Requests 1-4 are available from Chapter 2. Request 1 is Squirrel and Adult Rabbit; 2 is Cardinal, Rat and Woodpecker; 3 is Chipmunk, Oriole, Robin and Opossum; 4 is Songbird, Sparrow, Toad, Bullfrog and Skunk. All five are needed for 100%.

## Companion activities (Friends With Benefits)

- "Friends With Benefits" needs one companion activity in each camp chapter from Chapter 2 on, and each chapter's activities close when its camp moves. Chapter 2: Five Finger Fillet (Lenny), hunting (Charles), homestead robbery (Javier).

- "Friends With Benefits" counts the Chapter 2, 3 and 4 camps; the last window closes at "Banking, The Old American Art". Chapter 3: Five Finger Fillet (Micah), dominoes (Tilly), homestead robbery (Sean), fishing (Javier, Kieran), coach robberies (Bill, Sean) after "Friends in Very Low Places". Chapter 4: hunting (Pearson), coach robberies (Lenny, Micah), rustling (Uncle), and a bank robbery (Charles; Special/Ultimate Edition only).
