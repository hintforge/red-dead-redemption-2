# Red Dead Redemption 2 -- Exotics

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-10-30
**research_run:** P1 2026-10-30; P2 2026-10-30

Strand "Duchesses and Other Animals" with Algernon Wasp, a garden house north of Saint Denis. The request items are a plant and bird-plume checklist; world spots for the orchids are map images in the sources and are not in this file. [location inventory incomplete]

- Unlocks after the Chapter 4 mission "The Gilded Cage". There are five requests, 182 items by one tally; later requests stay locked until earlier ones are done. The final reward is Algernon's Revolver. The "Rare Orchids Order" note can vanish if you save and load before taking it from the post office. Saving Algernon in "The Gilded Cage" gives his business card.

Orchid regions (one rdr2.org guide): Acuna's Star -- east coast of Bayou Nwa | Cigar -- Bluewater Marsh | Clamshell -- Bayou Nwa and Scarlett Meadows | Dragon's Mouth -- wet lowlands of Grizzlies East, O'Creagh's Run | Ghost, Lady of the Night, Night Scented, Queen's (very rare), Rat Tail, Spider -- Bayou Nwa | Lady Slipper -- Tall Trees, Big Valley | Moccasin Flower -- Roanoke Ridge pine woods | Sparrow's Egg -- Roanoke Ridge riverbanks. No per-spot orchid list was read; an rdr2.org forum thread says the official guidebook omits some orchid spots. Collecting all but one of a type early can leave ordinary flowers at the last spot (reader comments only).
