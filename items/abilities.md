# Red Dead Redemption 2 -- Abilities

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-10-30
**research_run:** P1 2026-10-30

There is no skill tree. The abilities are Dead Eye and Eagle Eye, plus the three cores (Health, Stamina, Dead Eye) that set how fast the bars regenerate.

- Dead Eye slows time; you mark targets, then fire at all marks in a burst. It is upgraded progressively through the story, and later ranks show fatal points.

- Dead Eye rank 1 unlocks in Chapter 1 (mission "Old Friends") and rank 2 in Chapter 2 (the last part of "Pouring Forth Oil"). Ranks 3 and 4 come in later chapters, and which mission unlocks which is disputed. These unlocks come from search snippets, not from a page read.

- Fatal-point highlighting arrives with a later Dead Eye rank: rank 4 per GamePro, rank 5 per search snippets (a Chapter 6 unlock).

- Cores (Health, Stamina, Dead Eye) govern bar regeneration; cold or heat drains them; eating and sleeping refill them.
