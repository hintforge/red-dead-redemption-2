# Red Dead Redemption 2 -- Bounty posters

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-10-30
**research_run:** P1 2026-10-30; P2 2026-10-30

Sheriff-office and police bounty posters. Target locations are the nearest named landmark; no source gives coordinates. Counts disagree: 14 targets on PC per rdr2.org (11 on all platforms plus 3 PC-only), 10 per the German GIGA list, and 6 poster towns per PowerPyx.

- The 100% checklist requires 5 of 6 bounty posters. Poster towns: Valentine, Rhodes, Saint Denis, Strawberry, Blackwater, Tumbleweed. A third-party map shows 14 bounty-poster markers.
