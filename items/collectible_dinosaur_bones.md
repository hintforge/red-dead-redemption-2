# Red Dead Redemption 2 -- Dinosaur bones (30)

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-10-30
**research_run:** P1 2026-10-30; P2 2026-10-30

Strand "A Test of Faith" with Deborah MacGuiness. Rows are keyed on the nearest named landmark and the in-game map lettering; the source guides publish no coordinates, so none are given here.

- You mail the bone locations (not the bones) from a post office, wait about 24 in-game hours, collect the rewards and an invitation, then visit Deborah at Firewood Rise. Rewards: 1 mailed gives a Quartz Chunk, 15 gives a Skull Statue, 30 gives an invitation. The strand can be accepted in Chapter 2. Not missable.

- The strand is completable only after the story, because 8 of the 30 bones are in New Austin. The 30-bone reward includes the Jawbone Knife.
_source: https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/Dinosaur_Bones ; https://www.powerpyx.com/red-dead-redemption-2-all-dinosaur-bones-locations/ (P1 and P2 research 2026-10-30) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: high · enemy-tier: 3 · puzzle-tier: 3 · category: mainline · spoiler: story_ [Confirmed: 2 sources, 1 language]

- The last bone may not spawn if you collected bones before meeting Deborah; meet her, then return. The 30th bone cannot be inspected or highlighted with Eagle Eye until the first part of the strand is done. Bones vanish once inspected.
_source: https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/Dinosaur_Bones ; https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/A_Test_of_Faith (P1 and P2 research 2026-10-30) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: none_ [Single source -- verify · class:community-wiki]
> **Cross-system dependency** -- see `dependencies.md` DEP-004: meet Deborah and start "A Test of Faith" before collecting bones, or the last bone may not spawn.

- No bones are in Great Plains or Tall Trees; only two are real dinosaur bones (edmontosaurus, spinosaurus).
