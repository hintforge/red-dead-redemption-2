# Red Dead Redemption 2 -- Legendary fish (13 plus a bonus catfish)

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-10-30
**research_run:** P1 2026-10-30; P2 2026-10-30

Strand "A Fisher of Fish" with Jeremy Gill at Gill Landing. Locations are the nearest named landmark; no source publishes coordinates, so none are given here.

- Mechanism: meet Gill, then buy the Special Lake, River and Swamp lures ($20 each at the Bayou Nwa bait shop); mail each catch to Gill from a post office. A fish caught and then lost to death before mailing may vanish (comments).
