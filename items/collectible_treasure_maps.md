# Red Dead Redemption 2 -- Treasure hunts

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-10-30
**research_run:** P1 2026-10-30; P2 2026-10-30

Multi-map treasure hunts. Steps are landmark or picture puzzles (the hybrid localization class), so each step names a landmark. Puzzle dial 1 allows naming the hunt and its first map; later steps are request-based hints. [location inventory incomplete] Counts disagree between sources: IGN lists 11 treasure stashes, PowerPyx traces 6 chains, a third-party map shows 27 treasure markers; the 100% checklist needs one chain per one page and three per another.
