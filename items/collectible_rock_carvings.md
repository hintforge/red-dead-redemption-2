# Red Dead Redemption 2 -- Rock carvings (10)

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-10-30
**research_run:** P1 2026-10-30; P2 2026-10-30

Strand "Geology for Beginners" with Francis Sinclair. Rows are keyed on the nearest named landmark; no source publishes coordinates, so none are given here.

- Exception to the apparent rule: you can only log 9 carvings before meeting Sinclair; the tenth waits for the strand. There are no carvings in Lemoyne. Not missable.

- Either Arthur or John can finish the strand. There are no carvings in New Austin.
_source: https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/Rock_Carvings (P1 research 2026-10-30) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 3 · puzzle-tier: 3 · category: mainline · spoiler: story_ [Single source -- verify · class:community-wiki]

- Reward per the wiki: $10, Kentucky Bourbon, a Rock Statue and an invitation from Sinclair. A Spanish guide instead names a compass that crafts a talisman slowing weapon degradation; the two reports may both be true, which was not verified.
_source: https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/Rock_Carvings ; https://www.vidaextra.com/guias-y-trucos/guia-red-dead-redemption-2-mapa-video-para-encontrar-todas-rocas-talladas (P1 research 2026-10-30) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: none_ [Contradicted across sources -- see notes]

- Rock carvings are the fastest collectible strand to finish and so the fastest route to the "Collector's Item" achievement (see `items/collectibles.md`).
