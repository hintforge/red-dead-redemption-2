# Red Dead Redemption 2 -- Weapons

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-10-30
**research_run:** P1 2026-10-30

Weapon classes: revolvers, pistols, repeaters, rifles, shotguns, sniper rifles, a bow, throwables, melee and a lasso. The in-game Compendium tracks 59 weapons (48 needed for 100%); PowerPyx's list enumerates 65 including crafted throwable variants and account-gated items, so the totals disagree. Weapons degrade and must be cleaned (gun oil). Rows that name only a shop do not say which gunsmith stocks the item first (only Saint Denis is named for the M1899).
