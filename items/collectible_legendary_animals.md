# Red Dead Redemption 2 -- Legendary animals (16)

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-10-30
**research_run:** P1 2026-10-30; P2 2026-10-30

Sixteen unique animals. Locations are the nearest named landmark; the two guides read (Shacknews, English; GamePro, German) disagree in places, and both sometimes put a landmark under the wrong state, so the landmark is the key. No coordinates exist in either source. Spoiler note: existence of these animals is tagged `progression` (enemy dial 0 hides them until asked); the three animals of the far southwest are `story`.

- Mechanism: entering the animal's territory adds a map hint; track it with Eagle Eye clue markers. Each is unique and does not respawn. A legendary pelt lost before skinning is still registered at the Trapper (you lose the money but keep the outfit unlocks). Ten are huntable immediately, the rest are gated. The 100% checklist needs 5. If the "Legendary Animal Territory" notice does not appear, sleep at camp and return.
