## Honor

- Moral choices raise or lower Honor. High Honor gives store discounts and special outfits; low Honor gives more loot.

## Bounty and law

- Witnessed crimes raise a wanted level and attract law enforcement and bounty hunters; a surrender-to-law option and paying off a bounty exist. Returning fire at guards while witnesses are present still raises a wanted level.
_source: https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/Missable_Content_in_Redemption_2 (P1 research 2026-10-30) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression · entity: bounty · entity-status: unspecified_ [Single source -- verify · class:community-wiki]
> see reputation/bounty.md for full summary

- Robbing or looting $250 unlocks "Take From the Rich"; spending $5,000 across all shops unlocks "Pony Up".

## Hunting, skinning and the compendium

- Weapon choice and shot placement affect pelt and meat quality. "Skin Deep" needs every animal species skinned and "Zoologist" every animal studied (sub-species included); a few species that live only in a late chapter are excluded from both.

- The species excluded from "Skin Deep" and "Zoologist" live only on the story's island chapter, and those animals must be handled before that chapter's free roam ends.

## Camp and camp funds

- The gang camp moves between chapters (see `nav/architecture.md`). The Ledger, donations and camp upgrades unlock in Chapter 2 and end when the camp is left behind in a later chapter (see `nav/architecture.md`, edge E4).
_source: https://www.ign.com/wikis/red-dead-redemption-2/Camp_Guide_-_How_to_Upgrade,_Fast_Travel,_and_All_Upgrades ; https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/Missions_in_Redemption_2 (P1 research 2026-10-30) · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 1 · category: mainline · spoiler: progression_ [Single source -- verify · class:editorial-en]

- Honor missions (15 per the wiki) are optional missions that count for "Lending a Hand"; each has its own chapter window. The rule that Honor missions expire with their chapter has a documented exception: "Good, Honest, Snake Oil" lasts until a late chapter.
